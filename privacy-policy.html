STARFALL Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Effective November 6, 2026

This policy explains how STARFALL handles information. STARFALL is an offline single-player game. The game developer does not collect, receive, sell, or share personal information through the game.

Information processed on your device

Game progress, settings, and earned in-game currency are stored locally on your device. The game does not send this information to the developer or to third parties. Optional vibration feedback is handled locally by Android and is not used to identify or track you.

To erase the local game data, use “Reset Progress” in Settings, clear STARFALL's storage in your Android settings, or uninstall the app.

Network access and third-party services

STARFALL does not make external network requests and does not include advertising, analytics, account, social, or tracking SDKs. The app does not have online purchases or online leaderboards. Google Play and Android may process information independently under their own privacy notices when you install or use their services.

Children

STARFALL is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed to target children. Because the game does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data security and retention

Game data remains in the app's local storage on your device. An offline integrity check can detect some save corruption but is not tamper-proof. The developer retains no game profile or personal data.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes are released. The effective date above identifies the current version.
